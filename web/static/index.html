Monster Chess

PlayWatch engines

White has a king and four pawns but moves twice per turn. Black has the full army and moves once. You win by capturing the king; repetition or the 150-turn limit is a draw.

Ratings come from 7,680 engine-vs-engine games, anchored so v21 = 1600. Every 100 points is about a 64% expected score.

Drag or click to move. Right-drag draws an arrow, right-click highlights a square (hold Shift, Alt or Ctrl for other colours); a left click clears them. ← → step through the game.

Choose an opponent and a colour, then start a new game.
    Engine: self-trained neural network with Monte Carlo tree search, running on the host's GPU. Finished games are saved as move lists only.
